## Requirements
- **Four screens:** a **4K 144 Hz playfield** (main), backglass, DMD/topper, and a projector for the internal gameplay camera ([08](08-gameplay-camera.md)).
- Highest settings with headroom, and **VR pinball (Quest 3)** within 2-3 years.
- The motherboard lies flat in the cabinet with plenty of room around it.

## Key choices
| Item | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GPU | **RTX 5080** (vs RTX 5070 Ti) | 4K 144 Hz playfield plus three other screens at the highest settings, with headroom for VR later |
| CPU | **9850X3D** (from the video thumbnail) | |
